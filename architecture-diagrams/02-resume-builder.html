AI Enhancement

  • • enhanceJobDescription() — action verbs

  • • enhanceCustomEntry() — section-aware

Data & Export

  • • Import: PDF upload or paste text
  • • PDF: clone DOM → scale font → print()
  • • Markdown: generateMarkdown.js → Blob
  • • Limit: RESUME_LIMIT = 5
